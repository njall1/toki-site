Toki — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

Contact: vm2325.kids@gmail.com

Toki is a flash-card app for children aged 0–6 based on Glenn Doman's method. It is published in the Kids category of the App Store.

We collect no data. Toki has no accounts, sign-in, advertising, analytics, tracking, social features or chat. The app does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information about children or parents, and it sends nothing to us or to third parties. We have no servers.

Stored only on the device. Learning settings and progress — how many times each card has been shown, lessons per day and finished games — are saved only on this device. They are not synced to iCloud and are deleted when you delete the app.

Speech on the device. Words are spoken by the speech synthesizer built into iOS, on the device and without the internet. Toki does not use the microphone and records nothing.

Internet only for purchases. The app works fully offline. The internet is used only by Apple's App Store when an adult buys or restores the one-time purchases “Big Vocabulary”, “Read & Count” or “Unlock Everything”. Purchases are available only behind a parental gate. Apple processes the payment under Apple's Privacy Policy; we receive no information about you or your child, only Apple's confirmation that a purchase is active.

No permissions. Toki does not ask for access to the camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location or notifications.

Crash reports from Apple. If you have chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated crash reports and usage statistics. They do not identify you or your child.

Children's privacy (COPPA, GDPR). Because Toki does not collect personal information from children, no parental consent is required to use it. We do not process personal data within the meaning of the GDPR or the UK GDPR. If you write to us by email, we use your address only to reply and delete the correspondence when it is no longer needed. You may ask us at any time to delete it.

Changes. If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. The short version of this policy is also available in the app, in the area for parents.
