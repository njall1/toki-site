Toki — Support

Contact: vm2325.kids@gmail.com

Questions, ideas or a problem with the app? Write to vm2325.kids@gmail.com — we usually reply within a few days.

Restoring purchases. Tap the button for parents in the top right corner of the home screen, solve the parental gate (type the number written in words) and tap Restore Purchases. “Big Vocabulary”, “Read & Count” and “Unlock Everything” are shared with your Family Sharing group.

Ask to Buy. If a purchase waits for approval, the child can keep playing; the cards open as soon as the organizer approves it.

Card language. Words on the cards can be English or Russian whatever the language of the device: in the area for parents, choose Card language.

No sound? Toki says words aloud even when the Ring/Silent switch is set to silent. Raise the volume and check that Say words aloud and Sound effects are on in the area for parents.

A clearer voice. Toki uses the best voice installed for the card language. You can download an Enhanced or Premium English or Russian voice in the iOS Settings app: Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.

Privacy. Toki collects no data, has no ads or analytics and works offline. Read the privacy policy.
